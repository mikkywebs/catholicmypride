# Daily Mass readings data

Generated by `scripts/lectionary/build.py`. Do not edit by hand.

- `days-YYYY.json`: for each date, the celebration, colour and the keys of its readings.
- `passages.json`: the reading texts (Douay-Rheims, 1899 American edition; public domain).

Calendar and reading references come from the public-domain Tamil-Catholic-Lectionary
(github.com/jayarathina/Tamil-Catholic-Lectionary), using the General Roman Calendar (no national feasts), with Epiphany and Corpus Christi on Sunday
and the Ascension on Thursday.
